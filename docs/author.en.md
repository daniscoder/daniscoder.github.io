# About the author

My name is Danis Arslanov, and I am a seismic data processor. The programs on
this site grew out of my work: whenever a step took too much time or was easy to
get wrong, I wrote a utility for it. The ones published here may be useful to
others too.

## What I work with

- **Seismic data processing:** Omega, Vista, Geovation.
- **Viewing volumes and sections:** OpendTect, Petrel.
- **Survey design:** Mesa, Pikeza.
- **Maps and surfaces:** Surfer.

## How I write programs

- **Python and Qt** - histogram_for_reg, polygon, lmoToXY and autocrop are made
  with them. For large desktop programs - Qt and C++.
- **Delphi and Free Pascal** - earlier: [ListQC](listqc.md) and the first
  versions of [gPad](gpad.md) are written in Delphi, gPad 2.0 in Free Pascal
  (Lazarus).
- **Single-file builds** for Windows and Linux, including old systems such as
  CentOS 7.
- **Porting old programs:** I moved the velocity format conversion utility from
  the Omega package from Python 2 and Tkinter to Python 3 and Qt - about 200
  operations, builds for Windows and Linux. It is in use but not published.

## What you can order

- **A utility for your task** - a small program like the ones on this site: for
  the export format of your processing system, for the workflow of a field crew
  or a processing center. For Windows and Linux, a single file, no installation.
- **Automation of routine work** - scripts for batch processing of files and
  screenshots, format converters, data checks and cross-checks.
- **Extensions of the programs on this site** - a new input format, a new
  parameter, output for your system.
- **Localization of the programs** - the interface and help in English or any
  other language.
